Mel is a quantitative ecologist interested in all kinds of macroecological topics and patterns.

Mel has worked on deep time paleontological and neontological topics, including extinction risk factors (and climatic effects on them) in invertebrates and amphibians, geographic range size patterns, diversity, and macroevolutionary questions regarding the assembly of tropical rainforests.
