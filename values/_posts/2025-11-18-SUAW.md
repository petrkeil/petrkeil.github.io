We have started regular group writing sessions called "Shut up & write" every **Wednesday 9:00-11:00 AM in room D323**.

## What is Shut Up & Write?

The goal is to create space and time for focused, silent writing. By making this a group event, we minimize distractions and slightly increase peer pressure to prevent procrastination. The session should promote the habit of writing, and make the writing more structured.

By writing we mean **creation of a text that other people will read**. Examples are **scientific articles, essays, blog posts, popular text such as news articles, perspectives, opinions, rants, scientific reports, grant proposals, short stories, novels, or evan a poem**.

## What Shut Up & Write isn't?

This is not a time for getting feedback on your work, catching up with emails, running analyses, planning, brainstorming, coding, or working on revisions or edits of other people's texts. 

If you come to do these, then you may be losing an opportunity for the actual writing.

## What if I don't have a text to work on?

You can start a new one. Take an idea that you always wanted to develop in a text, and give it a shot. Revive a manuscript that needs finishing. 

## What exactly do we do?

Each session is focused on **2 hours** of focused, silent writing, with a **10 min break** in the middle. This is the structure:

1. Brief **welcome & intention setting** – participants share goals for the session (if they wish) and get settled.
2. Focused silent **writing**.
3. **10 min break** to recharge, stretch, and chat if desired.
4. Focused silent **writing**.
5. **Reflection & wrap-Up** – participants can share progress, challenges, or next steps.
