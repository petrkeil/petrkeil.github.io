On **September 9-11 2026**, MOBI lab went to **Ecology 2026 – 10th Conference of the Czech Society for Ecology (CSPE)** in Prague, Czech Republic ([conference website](https://www.cspe.cz/konference/ekologie-2026/)). 

These were our talks:

- **Mel Tietje**: Birds in sPACE and time: A process-based view of range dynamics.
- **Petr Keil**: Notable trends of biodiversity and its facets at local, regional, and global scales.

These were our posters:

- **Elisa Padulosi**: A cross-disciplinary framework for temporal dynamics across community ecology, landscape ecology, and remote sensing.
- **Flo Grattarola**: A sneak peek on RegRed, a new global database of regional red list assessments.
- **Gabriel Ortega**: Czech breeding bird atlas data: openly accessible biodiversity records collected over 40 years.
- **Adam Ulicny**: Pixels and pesticides: review of ecologically relevant agricultural datasets at large spatial scales.

Other MOBI lab participants: Kristyna Vlasakova, Carmen Soria, Kaca Tschernosterova, Safiya Praleskouskaya, Ivo Kadlec.
