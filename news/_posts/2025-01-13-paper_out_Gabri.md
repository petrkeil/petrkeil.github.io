**Gabri** and Petr, together with GRACE project co-authors, published a [new study in *Landscape Ecology*](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10980-024-02016-6). It shows that nineteenth-century land use shapes the current occurrence of some plant species, but it only weakly affects the richness and total composition of Central European grasslands.

This is the first major outcome of our [GRACE GACR](https://petrkeil.github.io/funding/post/2023/02/02/GRACE.html) project, and it's mostly thanks to Gabri's incredible work.
