title: European vegetation is becoming more dense and nutrient rich. Congrats Gabri for a new Science Advances paper!
author: Petr Keil

**Gabri** and Petr, together with their partners in the GRACE GACR-FWF project (Adam T. Clark, Franz Essl and others) have a [new paper in *Science Advances*](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/sciadv.aeb2493). Also, many thanks to Milan Chytry, Irena Axmanova, and others from Masaryk University, and to all of the many co-authors!

It's a huge synthesis based on >600,000 vegetation plots across Europe, sampled over the past 60 years, and a follow-up on our [paper in Ecology Letters](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/ele.70248) from last year. The most important result is a clear shift of plant communities towards nutrient rich and denser vegetation. Another major result is that wetlands have been getting drier.
