MOBI lab went for a weekend to [CZU cottage](https://janov.czu.cz/cs) in Janov nad Nisou (Jizerske hory). This was not about work, we spent the weekend cooking, hiking, making fire, and taking the advantage of a beautiful early spring weather, with bits of snow here and there.

We were joined by some significant others, and also by international members of Center for Theoretical Study and Antonin Machac's group.
