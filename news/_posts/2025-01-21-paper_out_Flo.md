title:  New data paper out - Big compilation of data on Neotropical carnivores. Congrats Flo!

author: Petr Keil
---

**Flo**, Kaca and Petr published a [new data paper in *Nature Conservation*](https://natureconservation.pensoft.net/article/140644/). It provides an analysis-ready dataset on distributional data on 63 species of carnivores in Latin America. Apart from the common presence-only data, it also compiles 45,468 presence-absence records from camera traps. These are invaluable in species distribution modelling as they allow to estimate true probability of occurrence.
